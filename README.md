# ☕ Premium DTC Coffee Subscription: Google Ads Search Campaign

Welcome to the comprehensive campaign repository for **BrewBound Coffee** (`BrewBoundCoffee.com`). This repository houses the documentation and implementation architecture for a premium Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) specialty coffee subscription marketing framework, completed as a Marketing Capstone Project.

---

## 📌 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Overview

BrewBound Coffee is positioned explicitly as a premium, third-wave specialty coffee subscription service. The core business model relies on delivering freshly roasted, ethically sourced, small-batch coffee beans directly to the customer's doorstep on a friction-free recurring schedule.

* 🎯 **Core Campaign Objective:** Acquire active, high-lifetime-value monthly subscribers and capture top-of-funnel email leads via newsletter sign-ups to fuel down-funnel marketing nurturing sequences.
* 💰 **Daily Target Budget:** $40.00 / day
* 📈 **Bidding Strategy:** Maximize Conversions (Automated Google Ads logic)

### 🎯 Audience Targeting Profile

* **Demographics:** Target core is adults aged 22–50, skewing heavily toward affluent urban professionals and remote technology/corporate workers who invest heavily in their home office environments.
* **Psychographics & Interests:** Third-wave coffee enthusiasts, advocates for sustainable living and ethical supply chains, home-brewing gadget hobbyists (Pour-over, Aeropress, Espresso), and productivity-focused lifestyle buyers.
* **Geographic Scope:** Nationwide distribution (United States) with heavily weighted bid signals pointing toward major urban coffee epicenters including **Seattle, WA; Portland, OR; and New York City, NY.**

## 🗂️ 2. Keyword Search Architecture

To ensure strict compliance with immediate e-commerce targets and protect marketing spend from being drained by informational queries, the selection strategy leans specifically into **transactional search intent**. Long-tail terminology isolates users who have already bypassed the awareness phase and are actively seeking to subscribe.

| Keyword | Match Type | User Search Intent |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `"Best coffee subscription"` | **Phrase Match** | **Comparison & Shopping:** Captures prospects researching options with active intent to join a plan. |
| `"Freshly roasted coffee beans"` | **Phrase Match** | **Quality-Driven Search:** Filters for buyers seeking premium freshness over cheap commercial alternatives. |
| `[Organic espresso beans]` | **Exact Match** | **Specific Product Search:** Maximizes relevance for niche, organic-focused espresso home baristas. |
| `[Whole bean coffee delivery]` | **Exact Match** | **Explicit Fulfillment Search:** Isolates high-intent consumers specifically demanding delivery models. |
| `[Single origin coffee box]` | **Exact Match** | **High-End Enthusiast:** Targets discerning buyers willing to pay a premium for flavor diversity. |

> 💡 **Keyword Match Type Justification:** By utilizing a balanced mix of Phrase Match and Exact Match parameters, the campaign establishes a protective perimeter against irrelevant searches. Phrase Match grants Google's algorithms appropriate flexibility to capture query variations containing our core phrases, while Exact Match locks down ultra-high-converting terms to keep Cost-Per-Click (CPC) efficient.

## ✍️ 3. Creative Copywriting & Responsive Search Ad Assets

In alignment with Google Ads Expert Mode guidelines, a highly modular layout was deployed for the Responsive Search Ads (RSA). This structure empowers Google's automated machine-learning engine to dynamically test and serve the most effective combinations of copy based on the searcher’s real-time context.

### 📋 Configured Assets Pools
* **Headlines Pool:**
    * `Headline 1: Freshly Roasted Coffee Beans`
    * `Headline 2: BrewBound Coffee Subscription`
    * `Headline 3: Get 20% Off Your First Box`
    * `Headline 4: Ethically Sourced & Delivered`
* **Descriptions Pool:**
    * `Description 1: Experience the world’s finest small-batch coffee delivered to your door. Freshness guaranteed.`
    * `Description 2: Join 5,000+ coffee lovers. Choose your roast, set your schedule, and never run out again.`
